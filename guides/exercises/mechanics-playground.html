SOLARFREN'S BEGINNER HANDBOOK · EXERCISE 1

Mechanics Playground

A small learning model: movement, stamina, and placing blocks. These are invented rules, with no commercial game files or online service.

1. Stamina and roll

Start with 100 stamina. A roll costs 20. One recovery adds 10, up to 100. A roll does not move the marker in this exercise.

Ready. Try five rolls, then a sixth.

2. Movement and blocks

Click an empty square to place a block, or a block to remove it. The yellow square is your player. Blocks prevent movement. You cannot place one on the player.

Use the buttons, or W A S D. Keyboard movement works when focus is outside the grid buttons. Rows and columns start at 1.

Ready.

3. Test and reset

Check: a sixth roll is refused; recovery stops at 100; a placed block stops movement; clicking it again removes it; you cannot leave the grid.

Changes exist only in this browser tab. Reloading starts fresh. No tracking, network requests, account, or saved personal data.
